Emmanuel Appiah Acheampong is a licensed architect and computational designer whose practice bridges biological intelligence, digital fabrication, and parametric design.

With advanced degrees from Cornell University and KNUST, and studio experience at the Technical University of Munich, his work investigates how nature’s logic can shape the built environment through emergent, data-driven form. He is skilled in architectural storytelling, diagramming, and communicating design intent across disciplines.

“Letting the logic of living systems become the grammar of architecture.”
