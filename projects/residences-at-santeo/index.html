Santeo, Accra.

A fast-growing residential suburb on the East Legon Hills.

Santeo sits in the Kpone Katamanso District of Greater Accra. It is known for modern architecture, serviced plots and gated communities.

The apartment

Depth, shadow, movement.

Vertical timber slats shade the glass, keep the heat out and leave the views open.

Timber screens move across a white concrete frame like wind through a forest canopy.

At the back, tall white arches lift the ground floor and give cars and people a sheltered way in.

The townhouses

Refined minimalism.

Cantilevered white frames create private, deeply recessed balconies.

White concrete, charcoal stone and vertical timber give each home its own character. Carports and partition walls keep the street calm and consistent.
