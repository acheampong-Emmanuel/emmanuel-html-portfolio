A place to arrive.

01 · The concept

Built around the experience of being here.

Arrival, gathering and retreat unfold around one shared heart.

The proposed scheme brings hotel rooms, food, events and leisure together on a long, landscaped site. A planted court and pool give the architecture its centre.

02 · Design logic

One site. Three connected settings.

The brief calls for comfort and a sense of belonging. The plan gives that ambition a spatial order.

The built programme gathers at one end of the long site. Parking and drop-off lead toward public rooms around a garden and pool; guest wings occupy the levels above. The event landscape remains open at the other end. Together, these spaces move from welcome to gathering to retreat, while staying connected to the outdoors.
