Living, with room to belong.

An apartment concept shaped around a generous arrival, practical homes and a more comfortable relationship with light and air.

The central entrance brings the apartment wings together.
The idea

A place that feels clear on arrival and calm once inside. The proposal gathers homes around a recognisable centre, then opens each wing to its own light and outdoor space.

The source presentation describes comfort and belonging as the design aim. This site makes that idea legible through the building's sequence: front court, stair circulation, then private rooms and terraces.
