Aerial view

A value chain that never leaves the property.

What does it change when nothing has to travel?

Live birds move from the farm zone to the processing plant on one short internal journey. No public road, no second handling, and a biosecurity line drawn once at the perimeter rather than at every gate.

Approach

One opening in the line.

Every vehicle, visitor and delivery clears security, registration, disinfection and the weighbridge at a single gatehouse off the Dompoase–Agogose road.

Threshold

Clean and dirty never cross.

Production sits west, processing and rendering east beside the road. Raw material and finished product run on separate aprons that never meet.

Release

A buffer, then home.

A 5.95-acre planted windbreak screens the workers' village from the line, so the people who run the site around the clock live apart from it.
