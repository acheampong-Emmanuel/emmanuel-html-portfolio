01 / The vision

A campus made for coming together.

What if every arrival felt like the beginning of something memorable?

The proposal brings events, guest accommodation and outdoor space into one walkable address. Each part can work on its own, while the whole campus supports a larger experience.

01

Approach.

A clear main road arrival leads to a landscaped forecourt and dedicated drop-off.

02

Threshold.

Glazed frontage and sculpted V-columns mark the passage from city to venue.

03

Release.

Shaded walks, courts and a rooftop pool extend the visit beyond the event itself.

Source notes and items to confirm

Content is based on the supplied client presentation and schematic plans. The deck gives two different total parking figures: 83–140 cars on the facilities page and 500 cars on the arrival page. It also describes the hotel as five-star while a separate plan annotation refers to a four-star hotel. Neither claim is used here. The grand hall is given as 1,200–1,500 guests in its detailed section and 1,500 in the summary; this site presents the range or the stated upper limit accordingly. The 150–600 figure combines the smallest conference hall capacity with the theatre's upper capacity. No contact details or video footage were supplied.
